# Deo Knowledge Base

**Website:** [deo.eljoy.tech](https://deo.eljoy.tech)
**App Name:** Deo AI

## Overview
Deo AI is your personal AI stylist that transforms your closet into a smart wardrobe. It solves the daily "What should I wear?" dilemma by digitizing your wardrobe and providing intelligent suggestions.

## Core Value Proposition
*   **Stop Staring at Your Closet:** Eliminate morning decision fatigue.
*   **Maximize Wardrobe:** Discover new combinations from existing clothes (3x more outfits).
*   **Smart Suggestions:** AI considers weather, occasion, and style DNA.
*   **Time Saving:** Saves the average person ~73 hours/year.
*   **Get 100 First Users:** Current goal is to acquire the first 100 users (Founding Members).

## Key Features

### Intelligent Wardrobe Management
*   **Snap & Identify:** AI identifies type, color, material, and style from photos.
*   **Smart Categories:** Automatically organizes into Tops, Bottoms, Shoes, and Accessories.
*   **Bulk Upload:**
    *   **Free:** Up to 5 images at once.
    *   **Premium:** Up to 20 images simultaneously.
    *   Features: Background removal, auto-cropping.

### Daily Outfit Suggestions
*   **Morning Routine:** Wake up to personalized recommendations.
*   **Context Aware:** Adapts to local weather and unique style profile.
*   **Style Intelligence:** "Why we picked this" reasoning.

### Event & Style Planning (Premium)
*   **Smart Event Analysis:** Automatically understands calendar events (e.g., "Dinner date") and generates outfits.
*   **Advanced Scheduling:** Plan outfits days/weeks ahead.
*   **Custom Rules:** Set personal rules like "No Jeans on Mondays".

### Learn Your Style
*   **Personalized Onboarding:** Build a unique style profile.
*   **Adaptive AI:** Learns from choices and wardrobe updates over time.
*   **Goals:** Define color palettes and fashion goals.

## Deo Premium Subscription
*   **Unlimited Events:** Automated generation for all calendar items.
*   **Enhanced Bulk Upload:** 20 items batch upload.
*   **Detailed Insights:** Advanced styling recommendations and reasoning.

## Target Audience
*   People who feel they have nothing to wear despite a full closet.
*   Busy individuals who want to look good without the effort.
*   Early Adopters: Looking for "Founding Members" to shape the product.
